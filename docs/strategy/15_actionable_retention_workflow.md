# TPW Actionable Retention Workflow Playbook

This playbook maps out the concrete, step-by-step retention workflows for wedding suppliers on The Perfect Wedding (TPW). It is structured into three main tracks:
1. **New Customer Onboarding Workflow** (Days 0–30: getting new suppliers to first value)
2. **Retained Customer Lifecycle Workflow** (Month 2+ through renewal: proving ongoing value)
3. **Behavioral Trigger & Churn Signal Workflow** (Automated & human interventions based on supplier usage signals)

---

## 📍 Track 1: New Customer Onboarding Workflow (Days 0–30)

This track is exclusively for **newly signed-up suppliers**. The primary goal is to get them to a complete profile (>80%) and their first profile views, clicks, or leads — the "aha!" moment where they perceive value.

```
[Day 1: Welcome] ───→ [Day 3: Photo Nudge] ───→ [Day 7: Progress Nudge] ───→ [Day 14: SEO Guide]
       │
       └─→ [Day 21: Social Proof] ──→ [Day 30: Month 1 Review]
```

### Day 1: Welcome & Setup
*   **Trigger:** Supplier account created.
*   **Action:** Send email: *"Welkom! Let's get your first booking"*.
*   **Content:** Step-by-step onboarding checklist, video tutorial link, and a call-to-action (CTA) to "Complete profile in 10 minutes."

### Day 3: Photo Upload Nudge (Conditional)
*   **Trigger:** Profile has `< 5` photos.
*   **Action:** Send email: *"Suppliers with 10+ photos get 3x more inquiries"*.
*   **Content:** Guide on selecting high-impact photos, showcasing top-performing profiles in their category.

### Day 7: Completion Rescue (Conditional)
*   **Trigger:** Profile completion is `< 80%`.
*   **Action:** Send email: *"You're 80% done — finish to go live"*.
*   **Content:** Visual progress bar, checklist of what's missing, quick link to direct editing page.

### Day 14: Initial Traffic Nudge
*   **Trigger:** Profile is live.
*   **Action:** Send email: *"Your profile is live! Here's how to get noticed"*.
*   **Content:** Basic SEO tips for TPW listings, category search trends, and a soft introduction to featured/promoted listings.

### Day 21: Social Proof Boost
*   **Trigger:** Live but no leads received yet.
*   **Action:** Send email: *"The secret of top TPW suppliers"*.
*   **Content:** Case study of a similar supplier who successfully booked weddings on TPW; profile optimization tips.

### Day 30: Month 1 Review
*   **Trigger:** Day 30.
*   **Action:** Send email: *"Let's review your first month on TPW"*.
*   **Content:** Dashboard link, first month's traffic stats, category benchmark, and a calendar invite link to schedule a free 15-minute optimization call with an inside sales specialist.

## 📍 Track 2: Retained Customer Lifecycle Workflow (Month 2+ → Renewal)

This track is for **existing, active suppliers** who have already completed onboarding. The goal is to proactively prove value on an ongoing basis and secure the renewal before churn signals appear.

```
[Month 2+ (Monthly Results on the 1st)] ─→ [Day -60: Renewal Prep] ─→ [Day -45: Loyalty Lock]
```

### Month 2 onwards: Proactive Value Proof
*   **Day 1 of Every Month: Monthly Results Email**
    *   **Trigger:** 1st of the month, 9:00 AM.
    *   **Action:** Send automated email: *"Je ThePerfectWedding resultaten voor [Maand]"*.
    *   **Content:**
        *   📊 Actual stats: Profile views, photo views, contact clicks, quote requests, shortlists.
        *   💰 **Projected Value:** Estimate value based on clicks (e.g., *"Your 8 clicks are worth an estimated €[Value]"* using category multipliers).
        *   🏆 Benchmark compared to top 10% in their category.
        *   💡 Quick tip of the month (e.g., response times, review collection).

### Months 10-12 (Renewal Phase: Days -60 to -1)
For annual contracts, secure the renewal before they begin questioning the €500/year expense.

*   **Day -60: The Value Recap**
    *   **Action:** Send email: *"Je abonnement loopt af over 60 dagen — dit is wat je afgelopen jaar haalde"*.
    *   **Content:** Year-to-date summary of views, clicks, leads, and estimated revenue generated on TPW.
*   **Day -45: The "Loyalty Lock" Offer**
    *   **Action:** Send automated email: *"Lock in your rate for 2 years (10% discount)"*.
    *   **Content:** Exclusive incentive to renew early, bypassing any upcoming pricing increases.
*   **Day -30: Inside Sales Phone Call**
    *   **Trigger:** No response to Day -45 email.
    *   **Action:** Personal outbound call from Inside Sales.
    *   **Content:** Relationship touchpoint, ask how the wedding season went, address any issues, and secure the renewal.
*   **Day -14: Urgent Expiry Reminder**
    *   **Action:** Send automated email: *"Last chance: your account expires in 14 days"*.
    *   **Content:** Visual countdown, highlighting loss of rankings and customer reviews if they churn.
*   **Day -7: Final Human Outreach**
    *   **Action:** Final phone call attempt from Inside Sales/CSM.
*   **Day -3: Grace Period Warning**
    *   **Action:** Send automated email: *"Your listing will be paused in 3 days — don't lose your ranking"*.

## ⚡ Track 3: Behavioral Trigger & Churn Signal Workflow

These workflows trigger automatically when a supplier exhibits specific behaviors that correlate with a high risk of churn. Some signals apply specifically to **new customers** during onboarding; others apply to **existing/retained customers**.

```
       [CHURN SIGNALS]
              │
              ├───→ [New] Profile Incomplete (Day 14) ───────→ Free Setup Offer
              ├───→ [Existing] No Login (14-30 days) ────────→ Re-engagement Nudge
              ├───→ [Existing] Zero Leads (45 days) ─────────→ "No-Lead" Optimization Offer
              └───→ [Existing] Visited Cancel Page (P1) ─────→ Outbound Phone Call within 24 Hours
```

### ⚠️ Signal 1: Profile Incompleteness (Day 14) — *New Customer*
*   **Signal:** Profile completion is `< 60%` on Day 14.
*   **Intervention:**
    1.  **Immediate:** Send automated email offering a free "We'll set up your profile for you" service (repurposing AM time to high-impact digital setup).
    2.  **In-App:** Prompt with a checklist popup next time they log in.

### ⚠️ Signal 2: No Login (Last 14 to 30 Days) — *Existing Customer*
*   **Signal:** Supplier has not logged into the dashboard for 14+ days.
*   **Intervention:**
    *   **At 14 Days (Yellow Alert):** Send automated re-engagement email: *"See what you've missed on TPW"*. Include a snapshot of recent couple searches in their category/region.
    *   **At 30 Days (Orange Alert):** Send personalized email from a named CSM: *"Hi [Name], I saw your profile views dropped last month. I have 3 quick ideas for you — can we do a 10-min call?"*

### ⚠️ Signal 3: Zero Leads (Last 45 Days) — *Existing Customer*
*   **Signal:** Active supplier has received 0 quote requests or contact clicks in 45 days.
*   **Intervention:**
    *   **At 45 Days (Yellow Alert):** Send email: *"Je profiel is bekeken — hier is waarom er (nog) geen aanvraag is"*.
    *   **Content:** Direct diagnostic checklist: "Do you have 15+ photos?", "Is your response time set?", "Is your description complete?". Provide a link to schedule a 15-minute optimization phone call.

### ⚠️ Signal 4: Visited the Cancellation Page (Critical P1 Alert) — *Existing Customer*
*   **Signal:** Churn probability model score rises above `0.75` OR supplier visits the `cancellation/billing` settings page.
*   **Intervention (Outbound Phone Call within 24h):**
    *   **System Action:** Auto-create a high-priority retention ticket in Inside Sales CRM.
    *   **Inside Sales Outbound Call Script:**
        1.  *Acknowledge & Listen:* "Hi [Name], I noticed you checked your account billing settings. I wanted to reach out personally to see how we can better support you."
        2.  *Diagnose the Root Cause:* Use the structured Menu below based on their reason:
            *   *Too Expensive:* Offer a **Downgrade to Basic (€299/yr)** or **Pause Account for 3 months** (freezes billing, saves reviews/rankings).
            *   *Not Getting Leads:* Offer **Free Profile Optimization** + a 30-day "proof period."
            *   *Business Slow / Seasonal:* Offer a **3 to 6-month pause** during the off-season.

### ⚠️ Signal 5: Completed Cancellation (Post-Churn Win-Back)
*   **Signal:** Supplier completes cancellation (mandatory exit survey filled).
*   **Intervention (Automated Email Sequence):**
    *   **Day 30 Post-Churn:** Email: *"We miss you — here's what changed at TPW"*. Offer: *Come back with 50% off your first 3 months.*
    *   **Day 90 Post-Churn:** Email: *"X wedding couples searched for [Category] in [Region] last month — you missed out"*. (Leverage loss aversion with actual search volume).
    *   **Day 180 Post-Churn:** Email: *"Major updates to TPW"*. Highlights new features (like real-time lead alerts) and offers a discounted reactivation package.
