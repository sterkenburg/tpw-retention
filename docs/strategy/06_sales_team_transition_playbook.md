# Sales Team Transition Playbook

## The New Operating Model

### Current State (Problem)
- Account managers driving across NL for €500/yr accounts
- No differentiation by account value
- Reactive retention (call when they want to cancel)
- No data on which accounts are at risk

### Future State (Solution)
- **Field sales** = Premium accounts only (>€2,500/yr, strategic value)
- **Inside sales** = Growth tier (phone/video, centralized office)
- **Digital/Automated** = Starter tier (self-service, chatbot, email flows)
- **Proactive** = Intervene before cancellation based on health scores

---

## Role Redefinitions

### 1. Field Account Manager → "Strategic Account Executive"
**Accounts:** Premium tier only (top 10-15% by revenue)
**Activities:**
- Quarterly business reviews (in-person or video)
- Expansion/upsell conversations
- Strategic partnership discussions
- Industry event representation
**Metrics:**
- Net Revenue Retention (target: >110%)
- Expansion revenue (target: >20% of book)
- Churn (target: <5% annual)
- Accounts: 30-50 max

### 2. New Role: Inside Sales / Retention Specialist
**Accounts:** Growth tier (middle 30-40% by revenue)
**Activities:**
- Phone/video onboarding for new accounts
- Proactive retention calls (triggered by health score)
- Renewal conversations
- Light upsell (Basic → Plus)
**Metrics:**
- Calls per day: 15-20
- Renewal rate: >80%
- Accounts: 150-200

### 3. New Role: Digital Customer Success Manager
**Accounts:** Starter tier (bottom 50-60% by revenue)
**Activities:**
- Monitor automated flows
- Respond to chatbot escalations
- Manage 1:many campaigns (webinars, newsletters)
- Analyze health score trends
**Metrics:**
- Health score improvement
- Flow engagement rates
- Accounts: 1,000+ (with automation)

## The "Red Account" Intervention Protocol

When an account hits health score <50 (Red):

### Step 1: Prep (5 minutes)
Open account brief:
- Account age, renewal date, current package
- Last 90 days: logins, leads, views
- Past support tickets
- Last conversation notes

### Step 2: Call Script Framework
```
"Hi [Name], this is [Your name] from The Perfect Wedding. 
I noticed you haven't logged into your account in [X weeks], 
and I wanted to check in — are you still getting value from 
your listing?

[Listen]

Based on your stats, you had [Y views] last month. Let me 
share 2 quick things that might help...

[Offer specific help based on their situation]

Would it help if I [specific offer: optimize profile / 
switch to annual / pause for off-season]?"
```

### Step 3: Offer Menu (by Churn Signal)

| Signal | Primary Offer | Fallback Offer |
|--------|--------------|----------------|
| Not getting leads | Free profile optimization call | Downgrade to Basic |
| Too expensive | Annual discount (20% off) | Pause 3 months |
| Not using it | 15-min "get started" tutorial | Pause account |
| Going to competitor | Match price + 2 months free | Downgrade |
| Business slow | Pause 6 months, keep reviews | Cancel with reactivation bonus |

### Step 4: Close
- If saved: Update CRM, schedule follow-up, send confirmation
- If lost: Complete exit survey, add to win-back flow, ask for referral

## Phone vs Video vs Email Decision Tree

| Situation | Channel | Why |
|-----------|---------|-----|
| First onboarding (Growth tier) | Video call | Build relationship, screen share |
| Red account intervention | Phone | Immediate, personal |
| Renewal conversation (Growth) | Video or phone | Negotiation, relationship |
| Renewal (Starter) | Email only | Economics don't justify call |
| Profile tips / optimization | Email + video tutorial | Scalable, referenceable |
| Cancellation save attempt | Phone (if reachable) | Highest save rate |
| Win-back (churned) | Email → phone if high value | Cost-efficient |

## Change Management: Bringing AMs Along

### Common Concerns & Responses

**"You're firing me / cutting my territory"**
> "We're upgrading your role. Instead of 200 low-value accounts that require driving, you get 40 strategic accounts where your relationship skills drive real revenue. Your commission potential goes up, not down."

**"I know my customers — automation won't work"**
> "For your top 40 accounts, you're right. But for the 160 others, they weren't seeing value because we weren't proving it. The dashboard and automated emails do what you can't scale: remind 1,000 suppliers every month why TPW matters."

**"What about the personal touch?"**
> "The personal touch is now reserved for where it matters. A €500 customer doesn't need a handshake — they need to see 3 leads in their inbox. A €5,000 customer needs the handshake. We're matching the touch to the value."

### Transition Timeline
| Week | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1-2 | Announce new model, explain "why", address concerns |
| 3-4 | Identify which AMs move to Strategic vs Inside vs other roles |
| 5-8 | Parallel running: old model + new flows, compare results |
| 9-12 | Full transition: field AMs hand off Starter/Growth accounts |
| 13+ | Optimize: refine territories, adjust targets, celebrate wins |

## Compensation Alignment

### Strategic AM (Field)
- Base: €60k
- Variable: €30k (OTE €90k)
- 60% on retention/renewal, 40% on expansion
- Accelerator: 1.5x for NRR >110%

### Inside Sales (Retention)
- Base: €45k
- Variable: €15k (OTE €60k)
- 70% on renewal rate, 30% on upsell
- Bonus: €50 per "Red account" saved

### Digital CS Manager
- Base: €50k
- Variable: €10k
- Based on: health score improvement, flow engagement, support ticket reduction
