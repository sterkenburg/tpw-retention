# B2B Retention Strategy — The Perfect Wedding
## Executive Summary

**Current State**
- Annual churn: **25%** (monthly ~2.3%)
- Average contract value: ~€500/year per supplier
- High-cost field sales model: account managers driving across NL for retention/sales calls
- Top churn reasons: (1) "Too expensive", (2) "Not getting enough sales/leads"
- Most suppliers do **not** track leads or use analytics

**The Core Problem**
TPW sells a "black box" listing. Suppliers pay €500/year but cannot see what they get in return. When budget pressure hits, the line item gets cut because there is no proof of value. Meanwhile, the cost to retain a €500 customer via field sales (Rotterdam → Groningen) destroys unit economics.

**Strategic Objective**
Reduce annual churn from **25% → <12%** within 18 months while cutting retention-related sales headcount by **40-50%** through:
1. **Customer segmentation** + tiered service model
2. **Automated value realization** (ROI dashboards, lead tracking)
3. **Proactive churn prevention** flows (email, SMS, in-app)
4. **Inside-sales + self-service** replacement of field sales for low ACV
5. **Pricing & packaging** changes to improve perceived value

**Expected Impact**
| Metric | Current | Target (18mo) |
|--------|---------|---------------|
| Annual Churn | 25% | <12% |
| Retention Cost/Customer | ~€200+ | <€50 |
| Field Sales Headcount | High | -40-50% |
| NRR | <100% | >105% |
| Annual Contract Mix | Low | >60% |

## The 4 Strategic Pillars

### Pillar 1: Prove Value Or Lose The Customer
> *"You can't retain what you can't measure."*

The #1 fixable churn driver is "not getting sales." Most suppliers don't track inquiries. TPW must become the system of record for every lead, view, click, and booking request.

**Key Actions:**
- Launch **Supplier ROI Dashboard** showing: profile views, contact clicks, photo views, quote requests, saved/wishlisted count, revenue estimate
- Auto-generate **monthly "Your Results" emails** with concrete numbers
- Add **lead notification system** (SMS/email when couple contacts them)
- Implement **revenue estimator**: "Based on your 45 profile views and 8 contact clicks, similar suppliers convert ~2 bookings worth €3,500"

### Pillar 2: Segment & Tier — Treat €500 Customers Like €500 Customers
> *"Not all customers deserve a Mercedes. Some need a reliable Toyota."*

| Tier | ACV | Motion | Human Touch |
|------|-----|--------|-------------|
| **Starter** | <€750/yr | Self-service + automated | Zero (chatbot only) |
| **Growth** | €750–€2,500/yr | Inside sales (phone/video) | Onboarding call + QBR |
| **Premium** | >€2,500/yr | Field sales + CSM | Dedicated AM, quarterly visits |

**Key Actions:**
- Move 70-80% of suppliers to **Starter/Growth** with automated retention
- Reserve field sales only for Premium + expansion deals
- Inside sales works from centralized office via video/phone

### Pillar 3: Automate The Retention Lifecycle
Build trigger-based email/SMS flows for every risk moment:

| Trigger | Flow | Goal |
|---------|------|------|
| Day 14, low profile completion | Onboarding rescue | Get to "aha" moment |
| 30 days no lead | Value anxiety | Show marketplace avg, tips to improve |
| 60 days no login | Re-engagement | Dashboard snapshot, success story |
| Pricing page visit | Deflection | Downgrade offer, ROI reminder |
| Cancellation request | Save + learn | Exit survey, instant offer, salvage |
| 60 days before renewal | Renewal prep | Annual discount, value recap |

### Pillar 4: Fix Pricing Psychology
- **Annual-first pricing**: Bill annually by default, monthly +20% premium
- **Introduce tiered packages**: Basic (€399), Plus (€799), Premium (€1,499)
- **Add value-added services**: Priority placement, featured badge, analytics pro
- **Grandfather existing** or offer "loyalty lock" to avoid shock churn
