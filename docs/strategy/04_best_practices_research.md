# Market Research & Best Practices Summary

## Benchmark Data: B2B SaaS/Marketplace Churn

| Segment | Annual Churn | Monthly Churn | Notes |
|---------|-------------|---------------|-------|
| **SMB SaaS** | 31-58% | 3-5% | TPW is here currently (25% is actually below median) |
| **Mid-Market** | 11-22% | 1.5-3% | TPW target for Growth tier |
| **Enterprise** | <5% | <1% | TPW target for Premium tier |
| **Top performers** | <5% | <0.5% | Requires deep integration + ROI proof |

**Key insight:** TPW's 25% annual churn is actually **not catastrophic** for SMB. But because ACV is so low (€500), even "normal" churn destroys profitability when served with high-touch field sales.

## The CAC vs ACV Rule (SaaStr / Tom Tunguz Framework)

| ACV | Sales Motion | Human Touch |
|-----|-------------|-------------|
| **<€1,000/yr** | Self-service + AI chat | Zero humans |
| **€1,000–€3,000/yr** | AI SDR + inside sales | Sales manager oversees AI, handles edge cases |
| **€3,000–€10,000/yr** | Hybrid: AI qualifies, human closes | Reduced headcount, higher productivity |
| **>€10,000/yr** | Human-led, AI-assisted | Full relationship sales |

**TPW Application:**
- Most TPW suppliers are **<€1,000/yr** → should be **self-service**
- Current model (field AM for €500 accounts) is economically impossible
- Move to: automated retention + inside sales via phone/video

## Case Study 1: Gorgias (SMB SaaS at Scale)
- **Model:** ~€3k ACV, 12,000+ merchants, approaching €100M ARR
- **How they made it work:**
  - Maniacally data-driven outbound (intent signals, tech stack triggers)
  - Intent-based prospects had **3-4x higher ACV** than cold prospects
  - AI Agent resolves 50% of inquiries autonomously
  - AI-powered selling inside the product
- **Lesson for TPW:** Use data to prioritize which suppliers get human attention. Don't treat all €500 accounts equally.

## Case Study 2: Yodle / Local Digital Marketing (S-1 Filing)
- **Model:** Low ACV local business subscriptions ($300-1,000/yr)
- **Retention insight:**
  - Tenured customers (1+ year) had **97.5% monthly revenue retention**
  - Platform product retention >> media product retention
  - Low acquisition + onboarding costs = rapid payback
- **Lesson for TPW:** 
  - Get customers to 1 year — retention skyrockets
  - "Platform" features (dashboard, analytics) retain better than pure "listing"
  - First-year focus should be aggressive on value realization

---

## Case Study 3: UK SaaS Platform (Churn Prediction)
- **Situation:** 850 customers, 18% annual churn
- **Intervention:** Gainsight PX identified that customers not completing onboarding within 14 days had **6x higher churn risk**
- **Actions:** Automated onboarding workflows, early CSM assignment, video walkthroughs
- **Results:** Churn dropped from 18% → 14.2% in 6 months, LTV +32%
- **Lesson for TPW:** The first 14-30 days are everything. Profile completion is a retention predictor.

---

## Case Study 4: Marketplace Vendor Retention (Shipturtle)
- **Common vendor pain points:**
  1. Financial: delayed payments, low order volumes, confusing payouts
  2. Operational: technical problems, clunky dashboard, inventory errors
  3. Support: lack of marketing support, unresponsive team
- **Solutions that work:**
  - Automated inventory/order syncing
  - Transparent analytics dashboard
  - Proactive communication about performance
- **Lesson for TPW:** Suppliers churn when they feel "in the dark." Transparency beats hand-holding.

## Best Practice: Tiered Customer Success

### High-Touch vs Low-Touch

| Model | Ratio | Best For |
|-------|-------|----------|
| High-touch | 1 CSM : 15-50 accounts | Enterprise / Premium |
| Low-touch (digital) | 1 CSM : 100-500 accounts | SMB / Growth |
| Tech-touch | 1 CSM : 1,000+ accounts | Self-service / Starter |

**TSIA Study Findings:**
- 39% of companies set low-touch threshold at $50k ACV
- 46% use 100:1 customer-to-CSM ratio for low-touch
- 28% of CS resources go to low-touch segment

**TPW Application:**
- Starter tier: 1 digital CS manager per 1,000 accounts (tech-touch)
- Growth tier: 1 inside sales/CS per 200 accounts (low-touch)
- Premium tier: 1 AM per 50 accounts (medium-touch)

## Best Practice: Annual vs Monthly Billing

| Billing | Churn Impact | Cash Flow |
|---------|-------------|-----------|
| Monthly | Higher churn (more cancellation opportunities) | Lumpy |
| Annual | Lower churn (sunk cost bias, fewer decision points) | Upfront cash |

**Standard practice:**
- List annual pricing prominently
- Charge **+20% premium** for monthly
- This is **not** a discount for annual — it's a surcharge for monthly

**TPW Application:**
- Flip default to annual
- Offer "monthly flexibility" at +25% cost
- Use renewal prep flow to convert monthly → annual

## Best Practice: Value-Based Pricing Psychology

**Research finding:** Higher prices can attract MORE customers when quality is hard to assess.
- $12.99 = mass market
- $15.99 = "no man's land" (too expensive for mass, not credible for premium)
- $18.99 = prestige shopper sees great value

**TPW Application:**
- Current €500/yr may be in "no man's land"
- Consider: Basic €399 ( stripped down), Plus €799 (current equivalent), Premium €1,499 (featured)
- Let customers self-select into value tiers

## Best Practice: Customer-Facing Dashboards

**Research:** Embedded analytics market growing to $200B by 2033. B2B buyers expect self-service data.

**Retention impact:**
- Customers who track ROI internally renew at higher rates
- Dashboards reduce "how are we doing?" support tickets
- Data becomes a monetization path (gate advanced analytics behind premium tiers)

**TPW Application:**
- Build supplier-facing dashboard as **retention engine**, not nice-to-have
- Show: views, clicks, inquiries, estimated booking value, benchmarks
- Send automated monthly "Your Results" emails

## Best Practice: Win-Back Campaigns

**Research:** Reactivating a former customer is **5x cheaper** than acquiring a new one.

**Brex case study:** AI-driven win-back increased booked demos by **40%**.

**Key principles:**
1. Segment by churn reason (price vs product vs business closure)
2. Reference original use case/goals
3. Show what's changed since they left
4. Use multi-channel (email, LinkedIn, phone for high-value)
5. Time with budget cycles / seasonality

**TPW Application:**
- Churned suppliers get automated sequence at day 30, 90, 180, 365
- Wedding seasonality: hit them in January (engagement peak) not November
- Offer: "Your competitors got X leads last month"

## Key Metrics to Track

### Lagging Indicators (Outcome)
- Customer churn rate (monthly, annual)
- Net Revenue Retention (NRR)
- Gross Revenue Retention (GRR)
- Customer Lifetime Value (LTV)
- CAC payback period

### Leading Indicators (Predictive)
- Profile completion %
- Login frequency (30-day)
- Feature adoption (dashboard, analytics)
- Support ticket volume/topic
- Engagement with monthly results emails
- Cancellation page visits

### Health Score Formula (Suggested)
```
Health Score = 
  (login frequency × 20) +
  (profile completion × 15) +
  (leads received last 90 days × 25) +
  (email engagement × 10) +
  (feature usage × 15) +
  (tenure in months × 10) -
  (support tickets × 5) -
  (days since last login × 2)

> 80 = Green
50-80 = Yellow
< 50 = Red (intervention required)
```
